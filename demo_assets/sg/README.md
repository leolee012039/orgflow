# Larkspur Bay Logistics (fictional, Singapore)

A company written to test OrgFlow on documents it had never seen: an org chart and three policies, in English and Singapore dollars. No company of this name turned up in a search on 13 October 2026; every page says it is fictional.

Two problems are written in on purpose:
1. Purchases above SGD 50,000 need "Regional Director" approval, and the org chart has no Regional Director.
2. Credit notes "Below SGD 1,000" go to the Customer Service Manager and "Above SGD 1,000" to the Finance Manager, so exactly SGD 1,000 has no approver.

Handoffs the documents state: Sales → Operations (booking confirmation), Operations → Procurement (approved purchase requests), Procurement → Finance (supplier invoices), Operations → Finance (proof of delivery), Customer Service → Finance (approved credit requests).
