# Wattle Creek Medical Group (fictional, Australia)

A group of general practice clinics in Brisbane written to test OrgFlow on English documents whose amounts are plain dollars ("$5,000", "incl. GST"): an org chart and three policies (purchasing, patient refunds, clinician onboarding). No company of this name turned up in a search on 13 October 2026; every page says it is fictional.

Two problems are written in on purpose:
1. Purchases over $25,000 need Chief Operating Officer approval, and the org chart has no Chief Operating Officer.
2. Refunds "Less than $500" go to the Patient Services Manager and "More than $500" to the Patient Services Manager and Finance Manager, so exactly $500 has no approver.

$5,000 and $25,000 (up to, over) are covered once; nothing should be flagged there.
